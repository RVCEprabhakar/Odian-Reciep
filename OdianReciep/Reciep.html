This is the recipe details

  • Chapati

    Ingredients for Chapati:

    • 2 cups of wheat flour
    • 1/2 cup of water
    • 1/4 tsp of salt
    • 1 tsp of oil
  • Chole Butter

    Ingredients for Chole Butter:

    • 1 cup of boiled chickpeas
    • 2 tbsp of butter
    • 1 onion (finely chopped)
    • 2 tomatoes (pureed)
    • 1 tsp of ginger-garlic paste
    • Spices: turmeric, red chili powder, garam masala
  • Chapati and Curry

    Ingredients for Chapati and Curry:

    • Chapati (see above)
    • 1 cup of mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, beans)
    • 1 onion (finely chopped)
    • 1 tomato (pureed)
    • 1/2 cup of coconut milk
    • Spices: cumin, coriander, turmeric
